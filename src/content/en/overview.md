# Time Density: project overview

In the source materials reviewed, Time Density is a project framework for describing how a system's state, transitions, and available actions relate to temporal context. Terms such as “density,” “phase,” and “transition” are project vocabulary; they are not, by themselves, defined measurements or validated physical quantities.

The framework may be explored as a conceptual model. Scientific or psychological claims require their own operational definitions, evidence, and citations. This overview does not assert a specific equation for density or a universal perception-delay interval.

Source: [`TimeDensity-Core/docs/concepts.md`](https://github.com/krv6246-cell/TimeDensity-Core/blob/main/docs/concepts.md).
