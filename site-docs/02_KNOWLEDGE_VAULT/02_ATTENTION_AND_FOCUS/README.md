# Attention and focus

This section contains a three-language introduction to sustained attention and the proposed Steel Quenching practice. The articles distinguish project language from established research: they do not present a fixed perception delay, a universal focus duration, or a clinical method as fact.

## Articles

- [Attention, focus, and Steel Quenching](attention_focus_en.md) · [ru](attention_focus_ru.md) · [it](attention_focus_it.md)

Steel Quenching is a proposed, adjustable working heuristic: **45 minutes of work, 2 minutes of break, and 10 minutes of integration**. It is not a productivity law or a requirement for every person or task.
